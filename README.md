# Customer Journey Mapping

## Project Overview

This project was developed as part of the **CodTech Digital Marketing Internship**. It focuses on creating a detailed customer journey map for **Nike** to understand how customers interact with the brand throughout their buying journey.

The project analyzes the different stages of the customer experience, including awareness, consideration, purchase, product experience, loyalty, and advocacy. It also identifies customer touchpoints, emotions, pain points, and opportunities for improving the overall customer experience.

---

## Objectives

* Understand the customer's journey with the brand.
* Identify important customer touchpoints.
* Analyze customer needs and expectations at each stage.
* Identify customer pain points and challenges.
* Improve customer engagement and satisfaction.
* Identify opportunities for better marketing and customer experience.

---

## Brand Selected

**Brand:** Nike

**Industry:** Sportswear and Fitness

Nike was selected because of its strong digital presence, large customer base, extensive product range, and multiple customer interaction points across online and offline channels.

## Customer Journey Stages

The customer journey is divided into six major stages:

### 1. Awareness

The customer first discovers Nike through advertisements, social media, YouTube, athletes, influencers, search engines, or word-of-mouth.

### 2. Consideration

The customer researches Nike products, compares alternatives, reads reviews, watches product videos, and evaluates price and features.

### 3. Purchase

The customer decides to purchase the product through Nike's website, mobile application, retail store, or other authorized channels.

### 4. Product Experience

The customer receives and uses the product and evaluates its quality, comfort, performance, and overall experience.

### 5. Loyalty

A positive experience can encourage the customer to make repeat purchases, follow Nike on social media, join loyalty programs, and engage with the brand.

### 6. Advocacy

Satisfied customers may leave positive reviews, recommend Nike to friends, share products on social media, and become brand advocates.

## Customer Pain Points

Potential customer challenges identified in the journey include:

* Difficulty choosing between similar products.
* High product prices.
* Limited availability of popular products or sizes.
* Uncertainty about product fit and sizing.
* Delivery delays.
* Complicated return or exchange experiences.
* Lack of sufficient product information.

---

## Customer Experience Opportunities

The strategy identifies opportunities such as:

* Personalized product recommendations.
* Better product comparison tools.
* Improved size and fit guidance.
* Personalized email and notification campaigns.
* Faster customer support.
* More informative product videos.
* Loyalty rewards and personalized offers.
* Stronger post-purchase engagement.

## Expected Outcome

The customer journey map provides a clear view of how customers discover, evaluate, purchase, experience, and interact with Nike over time.

By identifying customer emotions, pain points, and touchpoints at each stage, the strategy can help improve customer satisfaction, engagement, retention, and long-term brand loyalty.

---

## Conclusion

Customer journey mapping helps businesses understand the complete customer experience rather than focusing only on the final purchase.

For Nike, analyzing the journey across digital and physical touchpoints can help identify areas for improvement and create a more personalized and seamless customer experience.
